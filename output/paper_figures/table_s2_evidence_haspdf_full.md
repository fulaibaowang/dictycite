# Table S2 — Evidence-level R@1000 and MRR@10 with Δ[95% CI] on has-PDF and no-PDF queries (BGE-m3)

**Table S2.** Recall@1000 and BGE-m3 MRR@10 for abstract-only (Abs) and abstract-plus-chunks (+Chk) retrieval, stratified by abstract-evidence level and full-text coverage. Has-PDF queries have at least one cited article with chunked full text; no-PDF queries have none. In each +Chk column, Δ is the change from Abs in percentage points, with 95% paired-bootstrap confidence intervals (B = 10,000), matching the global significance procedure used for Table 1 / Table S1; where recall is unchanged per query the change is shown as 0.0. Adding chunks significantly improves the has-PDF queries (MRR Δ CIs above zero; recall Δ above zero for the core and insufficient strata) but significantly lowers ranking for the no-PDF queries (MRR Δ CIs below zero), which gain no body candidates (recall does not improve) while the added chunks push their gold abstract down. The no-PDF drop is significant but partly mechanical, since an article without full text can only be matched by its abstract. Counts are per stratum and may overlap across evidence levels. Per-reranker comparison across BGE-m3 / MedCPT / BGE-Gemma is no longer reported in the paper — see commit history / `report_7a.py` `_caption_table` outputs for those numbers.
